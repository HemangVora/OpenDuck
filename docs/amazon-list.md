## Order notes

- Order the **6804ZZ bearings first**: the listing showed "Only 2 left in stock" and you need both packs.
- The 18650 cells ship by Amazon's own ground logistics; no air-shipment problem, unlike the Hubtronics parcel.
- Most items are Amazon-dispatched (FBA) with free delivery only on a ₹499+ FBA cart; one combined order clears that easily.
- On the M2.5 screw page choose the **M2.5x8mm** size; the default variant is 4 mm.
- Set the FTDI board's jumper to **3.3 V** before touching the servo adapter.
