- **2026-10-30: decided to order everything possible from Hubtronics in one go; the built cart (25 lines, ₹50,433 incl. GST) is in `hubtronics-cart.md` with product IDs, substitutes and what Hubtronics does not stock.** Earlier plan: Hubtronics (servos, adapter, switches, Radxa, heatsink); Robocraze (IMU, buck, charger, BMS) delivers after 3 Nov, which is fine because none of it is needed until the pack build in Phase 2; move the FTDI adapter to Amazon so it lands with the servos. Amazon.in for the rest; ThinkRobotics + DigiKey only when vision starts.
- Amazon delivery dates above were read on 2026-10-29 for Amazon's default location; re-check with your Vadodara pincode. "FBA" items are free delivery only on a ₹499+ cart of Amazon-dispatched items, so batch them.
- Camera and ToF move to Tier 1 only if vision is wanted from day one; nothing in the walking phases needs them. Print the head face plate with the openings regardless.
- Radxa: the 2 GB variant at Hubtronics is the only Pi-Zero-footprint board in stock in India; importing lands near ₹5,500 after duty. 2 GB is enough for policy + camera + ToF.
- Bearings are 6804ZZ 20×32×7 per the official BOM (3 needed). Foot switches (4) are in the official BOM; KW-12 is a close fit, trim the lever if needed. No M2 screws: servos include theirs.
- Servo sources re-checked 2026-10-29: Hubtronics 11× ST3215 30 kg (was 33 on 10-27), 18× ST3215-HS; Robocraze 1× HS (₹2,283); Zbotic now blocks automated checks (had 19× HS ₹2,288 on 10-27, check manually as backup); Evelta sold out; ThinkRobotics pre-order 2–4 weeks; Amazon.in drop-shippers mid/late November.
- Why HS instead of a mixed order: 11× 30 kg for legs + HS for the head would work electrically, but it means two actuator models in MuJoCo, two BAM identifications and only one spare. The HS matches the stock 7.4 V servo's torque (20 vs 19.5 kg·cm) at twice its speed (106 vs 52 rpm), so the Playground reference gait needs no slowdown; the cost is more current per N·m (kt 8.3 vs 11 kg·cm/A) and about 10–15 % less battery time.
