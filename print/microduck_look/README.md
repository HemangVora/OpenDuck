# Microduck-look head for the Open Duck Mini v2 skeleton

New parts only. Nothing in `../3mf`, `../oriented` or `../../upstream` was changed.
Generated 2026-11-04 from the Microduck reference meshes (`~/Downloads/duck 3d .zip`) with `mdhead_generator.py`
(trimesh + manifold3d). Robot frame = the upstream STL frame: +x forward, z up, mm.

## What it replaces

| Upstream part | Replaced by | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `head.stl` (200 x 197 x 59 dome, 284 g) | `md_head_top.stl` + `md_face_plate.stl` + `md_cam_ring.stl` | loaf shape lofted from the Microduck top-shell profile curves |
| `head_bot_sheet.stl` (194 x 192 flat sheet) | `md_head_tray.stl` | 14 mm tray with the floor at the same height (z 109.7 to 112.7) |
| `left_eye`, `right_eye`, `bulb`, antenna holders, flash, speaker stand | nothing | the Microduck face has one camera eye and a ToF window; antennas and flash are dropped |

Keeps: `head_pitch_to_yaw`, `head_yaw_to_roll`, `head_roll_mount` and the 4 head servos. Still prints `6a_head_small.3mf` plate 1 for the three mechanism parts; skip the eyes, bulbs, antenna holders, flash and speaker parts on it.

## Interfaces that are identical to upstream (measured from the upstream STLs)

- Neck opening in the floor: 100 x 100 mm, centred at (x 20, y 0), same corner radius class, floor top at z 112.7. The yaw ring sits in it exactly as before.
- Roll-mount bosses: four, at (65, +-27.4) and (95, +-27.4), boss faces at z 158.4, 4.0 mm holes for M3 heat-set inserts, screwed from below through the roll mount's 5.5 mm counterbores. They hang from a 2 mm cage so the 32 mm towers print straight.
- Clearances checked in code: neck parts swept +-25 deg about the roll axis (x axis through z 129.1) stay >= 5.9 mm from the shell; the roll servo body >= 4 mm from the floor (same as upstream).

## Dimensions

Outer 200 (x -60 to 140) x 150 x 84 mm. Wall 2.5 mm, 3 perimeters. Tray 14 mm tall, floor 3 mm, registration lip 3 mm.
Face plate 3 mm at x 125 to 128, recessed 12 mm under the visor (7 mm at the bottom). Camera hole dia 16 at (y 0, z 156).
ToF window 10 x 10 at (y +22, z 156). Camera ring: 42 mm outer, 18 mm bore, 6 mm proud, glue on.
Rim screws: 9 x M3 (inserts in bosses that are webbed to the shell wall, 3.2 mm holes + counterbores in the tray floor). The tray carries a 3 mm registration lip that overlaps its own wall.
Face plate screws: 4 x M3, two into ceiling bosses, two into tray bosses (holes 4.0 in the bosses, 3.4 in the plate).

Mass (PLA 1.24, solid volume): top shell 167 g, tray 74 g, face plate 30 g, ring 7 g = 278 g (upstream head + sheet = 366 g).

## Not modelled on purpose

- Camera board mounting holes. The Ø16 hole clears a Pi camera lens or an M12 holder barrel; drill the board's 4 holes after you have the module in hand (IMX219 v2 boards use a 21 x 12.5 mm pattern, not verified here).
- ToF board holes. The window is 10 x 10; tape or hot-glue the VL53L8CX carrier behind it.
- Speaker and antenna mounts.

## Regenerating

`python mdhead_generator.py out_dir` in the scratchpad venv (`trimesh manifold3d scipy shapely`). Parameters are at the top of the file.
Print orientation copies are in `oriented/` (face plate and ring rotated flat). Slice with the CLI from inside the plate folder:
`--arrange 0` for the two big parts (they are pre-placed in the bed corner; auto-arrange rejects them), `--arrange 1` for the small plate.

# Microduck-look body shells (added 2026-11-04)

Replaces `body_middle_top`, `body_middle_bottom`, `body_front`, `body_back`, `battery_pack_lid`. Generated by `mdbody_generator.py`. Keeps `trunk_top`, `trunk_bottom`, both neck sheets and every hip part unchanged.

| Upstream part | New part | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| `body_middle_top` | `md_body_top.stl` | 45° shoulder chamfers replaced by R19 fillets; same 100 x 50 neck opening at (x -5, y 0); same four screw bosses |
| `body_middle_bottom` | `md_body_bottom.stl` | lower chamfers replaced by R12 fillets; same hip openings (no side wall for x -35..35); same floor pad under the trunk (top at z -89) with the 4 trunk screws at (+-10, +-10) and 6.5 mm counterbores; rear shelf at z -82 for the back flange |
| `body_front` | `md_body_front.stl` | same 10 mm plate and 8 counterbored holes, corners rounded, R6 edge on the front face |
| `body_back` | `md_body_back.stl` | fuller rounded rear (no taper) so a 3S pack fits; same 8-hole flange with ears; 3S bay: 62 mm channel, shelf at z -82, room for a 58 x 65 x 19 mm 3S1P 18650 pack plus a BMS up to 57 x 22 x 5 above it; rear lid opening |
| `battery_pack_lid` | `md_battery_lid.stl` | lid now on the rear face, flush in a recess, 4 x M3 into inserts, finger notch |

Every screw boss on the middle shells is tied to the side wall or the top by a 9 mm web, the back flange has ears around its holes, and the bay ribs stand on a full-width shelf: every part is a single solid (checked). Screw pattern front and back (y, z): (+-45, 17.4), (+-45, -22.3), (+-45, -62), (+-29.4, 33). 4.0 mm insert holes in the middle bosses, 3.5 mm through-holes with 6.5 mm counterbores in the plates (front from the outside, back from inside the bay, reach them through the lid opening).

Rear wall is 6.5 mm thick so the 3 mm lid recess leaves 3.5 mm. Checked in code against the upstream parts: no overlap with trunk, hips, neck sheets or legs; minimum clearances within 0.3 mm of upstream everywhere; neck opening, all 16 plate holes, 8 bosses and 4 trunk holes at identical coordinates; outer envelope identical (110 x 143 x 200 mm).

Plates (`3mf/`, one part per plate, Generic PLA, 3 walls, 15 % gyroid, tree supports):

| Plate | Part, orientation | Time | Filament |

## Filament

Sliced with Generic PLA. If you load Bambu PLA Lite, pick "Bambu PLA Lite" in Studio's filament box before slicing; the A1 profile exists (220 °C nozzle, 65 °C bed) and prints faster than the Generic preset. PLA Lite is a refill: wind it onto an empty spool first.
